In October 2026, our lab member [Yuyang He]({{< relref "/authors/YuyangHe" >}}) participated in the **4th Sino-French BioImaging Symposium (SFBS 2026)** in Bordeaux, France.

Yuyang attended the advanced imaging workshop and presented a poster on **world-coordinate-based 3D image registration**. He exchanged ideas with international researchers on advanced microscopy and computational bioimaging.
